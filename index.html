Key Findings
Borno Crisis
Borno State recorded 35,703 deaths — 48% of the national total — and 3,733 events (50% of all incidents). The ISWAP and Boko Haram insurgency has made it Nigeria's most dangerous state for three consecutive decades.
Civilian Targeting
19,538 civilians were killed — 1 in every 4 deaths. One-sided violence (deliberate attacks on non-combatants) peaked in 2014–2015 when Boko Haram carried out mass atrocities during its territorial expansion.
Multi-Front War
All three conflict types are nearly equal in total deaths. State-based 37.6%, non-state 37.7%, one-sided 24.7%. Nigeria does not face a single crisis — it faces three simultaneous, overlapping wars.
No End in Sight
The 2020–2024 average of 3,262 deaths/year shows the conflict has not de-escalated since the 2014–2015 peak. The frequency of events (400–630/yr) remains at historically high levels.
